General

How the app starts, stays up to date, answers the keyboard and where its core listens. Changes apply at once.

Startup

The core runs only while the app runs, so agents reach your endpoints only then.

Claude Desktop and Codex can call your endpoints as soon as you sign in to this computer.

Opens in the menu bar on macOS and in the system tray on Windows and Linux. The icon shows how many requests wait for you.

Updates

Signed releases from GitHub. An update installs when you restart the app, never in the middle of a call.

Once a day. You choose when to restart.

Reset GateRook?

Permanently delete all local application data. This cannot be undone.

All connections, saved credentials, settings, the local database, backups, activity history and installed plugins will be removed.

GateRook closes after the reset. The next launch starts fresh. You will need to reconnect your AI apps.

Your external databases and services are not changed.

Enter RESET exactly, then select Delete all local data.
