Secrets

How credentials are stored, encrypted and handed to a connector at call time, and where they come from.

Secrets are used only by the connector that needs them and are never sent to the agent

Nobody can view a stored value, here or through the API. To change one, replace or rotate it.

  • Paste the value once, or point to Vault, GCP Secret Manager or AWS Secrets Manager.
  • AES-256-GCM with its own data key, wrapped by the key in ~/.gaterook/master.keyset.
  • Decrypted at call time inside the connector and sent only to its own resource. External MCP servers keep theirs in their environment while they run.
  • Known secret formats and PII in the output become ‹masked› before the agent sees it.
