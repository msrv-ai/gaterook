How GateRook protects access

Your AI app connects to a GateRook endpoint. You choose which services and tools that endpoint can use.

Service credentials stay in GateRook. It checks the saved tool modes, requests approval when required, applies limits and records calls in Activity.

Connection switches apply across all endpoints. An endpoint switch pauses access only through that endpoint and keeps its saved configuration.
