Authorization

How AI apps prove who they are before they call an endpoint, and who can sign in to this app.

How authorization works

Every AI app is tied to one endpoint and can be cut off at once.

  1. 1AI app gets a token or signs in with OAuth

    Claude Desktop and Codex get a 15-minute token on the fly; Cursor, Antigravity and MCP Inspector send a named token; claude.ai signs in with OAuth 2.1 through the tunnel.

  2. 2Token is bound to one endpoint

    A token for dev can't call stage-readonly. Only its hash is stored.

  3. 3Policy decides: allow, approval or deny

    Each call is checked against the tool's mode and the environment's limits.

  4. 4Revoke any time

    The next call with a revoked token is refused. Nothing to restart.

See the tokens of endpoint dev

MCP access

Defaults for the tokens Cursor, Antigravity and MCP Inspector send with every call. They apply to new tokens at once; issued tokens keep their own. Claude Desktop and Codex get 15-minute tokens on the fly and need none.

Cursor and Antigravity keep their token in plain text in their config file, so a short lifetime limits what a copied file gives away. You can pick another lifetime when you issue a token. Nothing is written to the configs of Claude Desktop and Codex: the .mcpb bridge and the Codex headers helper ask the app for a token each time.

Roles

One role per person; Approver can be added to any role. Owners and admins sign in with two-factor. Nobody, in any role, can read a stored secret.

Owner
Everything, including people, the master key and audit export. Decides on approval requests.
Admin
Connections, endpoints, policies and settings. Can't manage owners or the master key.
Developer
Uses endpoints and Playground and issues tokens for their own apps. Can't change policies or secrets.
Approver
Decides on approval requests, from the app, Slack or Telegram. Added on top of another role.
Viewer
Reads Activity and Insights. Can't change anything.

How the tunnel works

Nothing opens on your network: the app dials out, claude.ai comes in through it.

  1. 1The app opens an outbound connection

    Cloudflare Tunnel or ngrok connects out from this computer: no open ports, works behind a VPN.

  2. 2claude.ai signs in with OAuth 2.1

    You allow the sign-in on this computer, and the token works for stage-readonly only.

  3. 3Same policy, approvals and audit

    Calls through the tunnel are checked, approved and logged like local ones.

Audit & retention

Every call is written to the audit log before it runs. Choose how long to keep it and where to copy it.

Append-only

Records can't be edited, and are removed only after the retention period and every export. A call that can't be written to the log doesn't run.

Retention

Older records are removed only after every export destination has received them.
