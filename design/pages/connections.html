How connections work

Add a resource once. Agents call its tools by name through an endpoint and never see its credentials.

  1. 1Add and test

    Pick a type, point it at a host and test it. The test shows what the account is allowed to do.

  2. 2Credentials stay here

    Encrypted with your master key, read from Vault, GCP or AWS at call time, or issued short-lived and renewed automatically. Write-only.

  3. 3Add it to an endpoint

    Its tools appear there as Off, Read only or Approval. Anything that writes waits for a person.

  4. 4Agents call it by name

    sql_query(connection='dev-db', …). Every call is logged in Activity.
