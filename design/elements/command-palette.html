Command palette

One keyboard entry point to the whole app: go to a page, run an action, or open a connection or an endpoint by name without leaving the keyboard. Opens from every page with ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) or the Search button in the titlebar.

Open with ⌘K

The real modal, driven by kit.js alone (no page script): press ⌘K or Ctrl+K, or use the button. Type to filter every group at once; Up and Down (wrapping), Home and End move the active option (a wash, an inset ring and the return-key icon), Enter opens it or runs its action, Esc closes and returns focus here. Focus never leaves the search field. Go to lists every page, with its sidebar group as the hint.
