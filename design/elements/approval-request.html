Pending: the action contains hidden characters

Verbatim is not enough when a character can't be seen. Bidi overrides, zero-width characters, tabs and trailing spaces render as boxed escapes (gw-tok-invisible); a look-alike non-ASCII letter outside a string is boxed as itself (gw-tok-confusable). A warning above the block counts and names them. Long lines wrap, and a wrapped line hangs under its first line.
