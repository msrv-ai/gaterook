A KPI tile answers one question at a glance on Overview and Insights: a label, the value with its unit, how it changed and, if useful, a target or a sparkline. The change always carries an icon and words with a sign or direction ("+12% vs yesterday"); its colour comes from what the change means (data-sentiment), not from its direction. A row of tiles is one <dl>. Do not use a stat for a value without context or comparison: a key-value list says it more quietly.
