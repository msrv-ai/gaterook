Empty states gw-empty

What will appear here and the one action that fills it. Three situations: a first run (nothing configured yet), a query with no results (filters too narrow) and all clear (nothing waiting). The title states the situation, the text explains what appears here and why it matters, and there is one action at most. Do not use an empty state for a failure: say what happened and what to do in a danger alert.
