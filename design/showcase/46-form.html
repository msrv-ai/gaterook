Form layout gw-form

Sections of fields, each with a title and one paragraph that says what it controls. Below 1280px everything stacks; from lg the title and description take the left third and the fields the right two thirds. A section is a fieldset whose legend names it; fields that belong together sit side by side in gw-form__row. Forms that save on a button use --sticky-actions: while there are unsaved changes, "Unsaved changes" with Discard and Save floats at the bottom of the scrolling area. Settings that apply at once use gw-switch and need no Save. (The demo form uses method="dialog" so Save does not reload the showcase.)
