Skeleton gw-skeleton

Placeholder in the shape of content that is about to arrive, for the first render of a list, a card or a chart when the wait passes about 300 ms. A calm opacity pulse, never a shimmer sweep, at half speed with reduced motion. The blocks are aria-hidden; the region carries aria-busy="true" and an sr-only status names what is loading. Use a spinner for in-place actions (Test connection), gw-progress for long jobs, and never a skeleton for content that failed: show the error with gw-alert--danger.
