Toasts and alerts gw-toast gw-alert

A toast reports the result of something the user just did, or an event that needs no answer now, and leaves after 6 s. It is never the only place a decision or an error lives: approvals stay in Approvals, a failing connection keeps its alert. Reversible actions show a toast with Undo instead of a confirmation. An alert describes a state that holds while it is true: inline in a section or form, or as a banner above the page for app-wide state (Core stopped, Tunnel is public). Both say what happened and what to do; the tone is never the only cue, the icon and the words say it too.
