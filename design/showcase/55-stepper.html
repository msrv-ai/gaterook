Stepper gw-stepper

Where the user is in a short linear flow of three to five steps: Add connection, first-run onboarding. With --guide it is a two- or three-step first-run guide whose steps carry their own actions. An ordered list; the current step carries aria-current="step", and completed or failed steps say so in sr-only text, not only with the check or the cross. Horizontal from 960px, vertical below it or always with --vertical. It shows progress, it is not navigation: going back is a Back button. Do not use it for views of equal weight (gw-tabs) or for an open-ended checklist (getting-started).
