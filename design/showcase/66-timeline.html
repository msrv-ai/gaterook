Timeline gw-timeline

A vertical feed of what happened, in order: the path of an approval request from request to execution, the steps of an agent trace. It is an <ol>; each item has a toned marker with an icon from the decision vocabulary, a title that says what happened in words, meta, and a <time datetime>. The step a request is in right now carries aria-current="step": filled marker and a heavier title. Use it for history; for the steps of a form or wizard use gw-stepper.
