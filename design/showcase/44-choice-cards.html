Choice cards gw-choice-card

A radio or checkbox drawn as a card, for choices that need an icon and a sentence: connection type, secret source, environment. The native input covers the card, so a click anywhere selects it and arrow keys move between radio cards. Checked = 2px brand border, brand tint and a filled indicator with a check; keyboard focus rings the whole card. Name the input with aria-labelledby (title) and aria-describedby (description) when the card has more than a title. Lay cards out with gw-grid inside fieldset.gw-field. For one-word options use gw-segmented; for options that need no explanation use gw-radio.
