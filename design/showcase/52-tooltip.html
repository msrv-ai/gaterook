Tooltip gw-tooltip

A short, non-essential hint next to its trigger: the name of an icon-only button, the absolute time behind "3 min ago", why a control is unavailable. CSS only: it shows on hover (after a beat) and at once on keyboard focus, and the pointer can move onto it. When the tooltip is the name, point aria-labelledby at it; when it adds information, use aria-describedby. Never put links, buttons or anything needed to finish a task in a tooltip (it is not interactive and not reachable by touch): use a popover or inline text instead.
