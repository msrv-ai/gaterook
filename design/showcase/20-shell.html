App shell gw-shell gw-titlebar gw-sidebar gw-nav

The one window frame of the app: titlebar across the top, sidebar on the left, main on the right. The window never scrolls as a whole; the sidebar body and main scroll on their own. Every page copies the shell verbatim from pages/_shell.html and changes only the title, aria-current and the page content. Do not build a second navigation, a page-level sidebar or a custom header: sub-navigation inside a page is gw-tabs, settings groups are the settings-layout element.

Expanded and collapsed

Left: the default 240px sidebar. Right: .gw-shell[data-collapsed], set by the panel button (data-gw-sidebar-toggle; kit.js keeps aria-expanded in sync and remembers the choice across pages) and applied automatically below 960px. Collapsed, labels stay in the accessibility tree, groups are split by hairlines, the Approvals counter becomes an amber dot on the icon, and hovering or tabbing to an icon shows its label beside it. Markup: copy pages/_shell.html.

Without sidebar

Onboarding and the core status screen use the same frame without gw-sidebar: main spans the full width under the titlebar, usually with a gw-page--narrow inside. Everything app-wide that is not navigation, such as a gw-alert--banner, goes first inside gw-shell__main, never as a fourth child of the grid.

Titlebar

The whole bar is an Electron drag region; links, buttons, labels and popovers inside opt out automatically. gw-titlebar--mac (second bar) reserves the traffic-light inset plus a small gap before Back; on Windows and Linux the Window Controls Overlay area is reserved through env(titlebar-area-*). The section title stays centered in the window and truncates before the controls. Back is disabled until an earlier application page exists; its enabled and disabled states are shown below. Actions: search with its shortcut, the approvals bell with an attention counter, the appearance popover (theme and contrast). The popover title is not a heading: the titlebar comes before the page h1.

Sidebar and navigation

Items are links with aria-current="page" on the current one: selected fill, primary text, semibold and a 2px brand bar, so the state never rests on colour alone. Groups follow the questions the app answers (Resources: what is connected, Access: who gets in and how, Observe: what agents did) and are role="group" labelled by their overline. Settings sits at the bottom. The footer always shows the local core and the tunnel, in words; the core dot stays still (never gw-status--live here: a dot that pulses on every screen forever fails WCAG 2.2.2).
