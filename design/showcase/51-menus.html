Menus and popovers gw-menu-host gw-menu gw-popover

The kit's only dropdown. A menu holds actions on one object; a listbox picks one value and shows a check; a popover holds a small form next to its trigger (add a filter). All three open on click, Enter or Space, never on hover or focus. Arrows move, Home and End jump, Esc closes and returns focus to the trigger, Tab or a click outside closes. A danger item never acts by itself: it opens a confirmation. Do not use a menu to move between pages, to hide the one main action of a view, or to choose between two or three always-visible options (gw-segmented).
