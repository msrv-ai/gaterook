Waterfall gw-waterfall

Where the time of one call went: the gateway's own work (authentication, policy, secret, masking), time inside the resource, and the idle wait for a human decision. Rows are phases in order (<ol>); each reads as "phase, duration", and the bar is decoration. Spans are placed with --start and --len as fractions of the total and are never thinner than 2px. Use it in event details; for latency across many calls use a chart.
