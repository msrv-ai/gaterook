Badges and counters gw-badge gw-counter

A badge names a state in a word or two: the decision on a call, the lifetime of a token, the kind of a connector. The tone, icon and words come from one vocabulary (BLUEPRINT §7), so "Denied by policy" looks the same in Activity, Approvals and Insights. Badges are display only: a removable value or an active filter is a tag, an environment is the env marker, a toggle is a pressed button. A counter is a bare number next to a nav item, tab or button; amber means a human is waiting.

Counters

A number alone means little to a screen reader: add hidden context ("3 waiting") or make sure the control's name says what is counted. Default is a plain count, attention (amber) means a human is waiting, muted is a quiet count beside a label.
