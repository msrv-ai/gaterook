Native questions and answers

Use native details and summary for optional explanations. The summary is keyboard accessible without JavaScript. Keep the local-processing boundary explicit: a cloud AI can receive permitted tool results.

What stays on my computer?

GateRook runs locally and manages your connection secrets and access rules on your computer. If you use a cloud AI, permitted tool results may be sent to that AI provider.

Can the AI change production without me?

Changes require your approval. You review the exact action and target environment before deciding whether it should run. You can also make tools read only or turn them off.
