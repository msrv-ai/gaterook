Dialogs and drawers gw-dialog gw-drawer

Native <dialog> opened with showModal(): focus moves in and stays, Esc closes, the page behind is inert, and focus returns to the opener. A dialog asks one question or holds one short task: confirm a revoke, create an endpoint. A drawer slides in from the right to read beside the list: the detail of an event, a trace. Do not use a dialog for content people compare with the page (use a drawer or gw-split), for long multi-step setup (a page with gw-stepper) or to report success (gw-toast). Every dialog names its initial focus in the markup: autofocus on the first field of a form, or on Cancel in a confirmation (danger and warning alike), so Enter never confirms by accident.
