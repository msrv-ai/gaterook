Status, environment, mode gw-status gw-env gw-mode

Three small markers that answer "is it working", "where does it run" and "what may the agent do". Status is a dot and words for the health of a connection, the core, the tunnel or an AI app; the words always say the state, the dot is the second cue. Env marks dev, stage or prod next to a connection name in every risky context; its shape (straight bar, mono capitals) keeps it apart from a status pill. Mode shows a tool's mode where it cannot be changed; to choose a mode use the segmented control. Do not use a status dot for a decision on a call: that is a badge.
