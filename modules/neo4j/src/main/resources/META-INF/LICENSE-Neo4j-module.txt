This package is GateRook's Neo4j connector with the libraries it needs, unmodified, in one file.

GateRook's own code (ai.msrv.gaterook.connectors.*, https://github.com/gaterook/gaterook) is under the Apache
License 2.0.

It includes:

- Neo4j Java Driver @neo4j-java-driver.version@ (org.neo4j.driver:neo4j-java-driver, with
  org.neo4j.bolt:neo4j-bolt-connection and its codec, netty, pooled and routed parts;
  https://github.com/neo4j/neo4j-java-driver), under the Apache License 2.0.
- Netty (io.netty:netty-handler, netty-codec-base, netty-transport, netty-transport-native-unix-common, netty-buffer,
  netty-resolver, netty-common and netty-tcnative-classes; https://netty.io), under the Apache License 2.0.
- Reactor Core (io.projectreactor:reactor-core, https://projectreactor.io), under the Apache License 2.0. Its license
  is META-INF/LICENSE.
- Reactive Streams (org.reactivestreams:reactive-streams, https://www.reactive-streams.org), under the MIT No
  Attribution license.
- JSpecify (org.jspecify:jspecify, https://jspecify.dev), under the Apache License 2.0.
- OkHttp @okhttp.version@ (com.squareup.okhttp3:okhttp-jvm and okhttp-tls) and Okio (com.squareup.okio:okio-jvm;
  https://square.github.io), under the Apache License 2.0.
- The Kotlin standard library (org.jetbrains.kotlin:kotlin-stdlib) and JetBrains annotations
  (org.jetbrains:annotations; https://kotlinlang.org), under the Apache License 2.0.

The text of the Apache License 2.0 is META-INF/LICENSE.
