This package is GateRook's Apache Cassandra connector with the libraries it needs, unmodified, in one file.

GateRook's own code (ai.msrv.gaterook.connectors.*, https://github.com/gaterook/gaterook) is under the Apache
License 2.0.

It includes:

- Apache Cassandra Java Driver @cassandra-driver.version@ (org.apache.cassandra:java-driver-core and
  java-driver-guava-shaded, with com.datastax.oss:native-protocol; https://github.com/apache/cassandra-java-driver),
  under the Apache License 2.0. Its license is META-INF/LICENSE and its notices are META-INF/NOTICE.txt and
  META-INF/NOTICE_binary.txt.
- Netty (io.netty:netty-handler, netty-codec-base, netty-transport, netty-transport-native-unix-common, netty-buffer,
  netty-resolver and netty-common; https://netty.io), under the Apache License 2.0.
- Typesafe Config (com.typesafe:config, https://github.com/lightbend/config), under the Apache License 2.0.
- Reactive Streams (org.reactivestreams:reactive-streams, https://www.reactive-streams.org), under the MIT No
  Attribution license.
- OkHttp @okhttp.version@ (com.squareup.okhttp3:okhttp-jvm and okhttp-tls) and Okio (com.squareup.okio:okio-jvm;
  https://square.github.io), under the Apache License 2.0.
- The Kotlin standard library (org.jetbrains.kotlin:kotlin-stdlib) and JetBrains annotations
  (org.jetbrains:annotations; https://kotlinlang.org), under the Apache License 2.0.
